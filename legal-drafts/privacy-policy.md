## Enquiries and local downloads

The consultation form uses your name, email address, optional company name, area of interest and project message to prepare an email addressed to hello@nexadly.com. You review and send that email in your chosen email application. The website itself does not send the form to a Nexadly server or save it to an enquiry database.

The download button creates a text file in your browser. Downloading it does not deliver it to Nexadly. Your device, email app, autofill and backups may retain copies according to your settings.

If you send an email, the delivered message can include your contact details, message, attachments and normal email headers. Do not include passwords, payment-card details or unnecessary sensitive information in an initial enquiry.

## Hosting, fonts and cookies

The current preview uses ChatGPT Sites and may require platform authentication. Nexadly’s site code does not implement its own login or request your ChatGPT password. Platform operation may involve technical request information and cookies. Consult [OpenAI’s Privacy Policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/) and [Cookie Policy](https://openai.com/policies/cookie-policy/); these do not establish a complete site-specific inventory.

The stylesheet requests Google Fonts. Font requests expose the requesting IP address to Google. See [Google’s font privacy explanation](https://fonts.googleblog.com/2022/11/your-privacy-and-google-fonts.html).

This version’s Nexadly application code does not set cookies, write enquiry data to browser storage or embed a custom analytics script or advertising pixel. This code finding is not a claim that all hosting or third-party processing is absent. See the [Cookie Policy draft](/cookies/).

> **Review required:** Confirm the production host, site-specific technical logs, platform cookie inventory, recipients and processing locations. Review Google Fonts delivery and whether to self-host fonts before public launch. Update this text when the host, form, analytics, pixel or communication integrations change.

## Retention, security and international processing

Downloads and unsent drafts remain under the visitor’s device and email-app controls. Delivered correspondence may be stored in the recipient mailbox and its provider systems. No retention duration or storage location has yet been approved for this draft.

Provider processing may occur outside the visitor’s country. The actual provider locations and processing arrangements must be verified independently.

> **Review required:** Approve a data inventory and retention schedule for enquiries, contracts, billing, requests, logs and backups; record deletion methods and any justified retention requirements. Verify access controls, incident procedures, provider contracts, international transfer requirements and locations. Do not claim encryption, certifications, security guarantees or country-specific hosting without evidence.
