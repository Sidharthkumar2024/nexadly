## Before a customer commits

A written proposal should identify the contracting entity, deliverables, dependencies, total price in US dollars (USD), payment stages, taxes and separately payable third-party costs. It should also explain cancellation and refund arrangements before payment is requested.

> **Review required:** Approve the actual project pricing and payment process. Confirm what is delivered at each milestone, how acceptance works, any deposits, and the distinction between agreed work and unused advance amounts. No default refund percentage or refund period is supplied.

## Proposed cancellation request process

Proposed contact route: email [hello@nexadly.com](mailto:hello@nexadly.com) with “Project cancellation request”, the project reference if available and enough context to identify the engagement. Do not include a full payment-card number or account credentials.

The proposed process is to confirm the request’s scope, review work and costs against the agreement, and explain the next steps in writing. Merely sending a request should not be described as instantly cancelling all obligations or stopping third-party billing.

> **Review required:** Approve who handles requests, how receipt and authorization are checked, notice requirements, work-pause rules, and any applicable response deadlines. Decide how partially completed work, approved changes and handover are treated.

## Proposed refund assessment

Any refund assessment should consider the specific agreement, what was paid, the work supplied, the reason for the request and applicable rights. This draft sets no universal non-refundable rule, discretionary guarantee or automatic entitlement.

Third-party subscriptions, domains, advertising spend and provider fees may have separate supplier terms. Their treatment must be identified before purchase and checked for the particular case; they are not declared non-refundable here.

> **Review required:** Approve refund eligibility, calculation, authorization, payment method, currency-conversion treatment, processing timing and third-party cost handling. Confirm how mandatory cancellation, consumer and other legal rights affect the final policy; do not state that a contract removes such rights.
