## Proposed request route

For corporate correspondence, email [hello@nexadly.com](mailto:hello@nexadly.com) with “Data deletion request”. Include the email address used for the enquiry, an approximate date or subject and what you want removed. Send only the information needed to locate the records; do not attach identity documents, passwords or payment-card details in the first message.

A request can also identify an authorized representative where applicable. Any further identity or authority check should be proportionate to the risk of disclosing or deleting the wrong person’s information.

> **Review required:** Approve the monitored inbox, responsible person, identity checks, authorization requirements, request register and applicable legal response periods. Do not collect identity documentation routinely when a less intrusive check is sufficient.

## Proposed assessment and outcome

The proposed workflow is to locate relevant corporate records, assess the request under applicable law, determine what can be deleted and explain the outcome. If more context is needed, request only what is necessary.

Where a specific legal retention obligation or other valid exception applies, the final process should identify the affected information, purpose and limit of retention, and explain available next steps where required. This draft does not authorize blanket retention or promise that all data can always be erased.

> **Review required:** Confirm relevant laws, permitted exceptions, required explanations and complaint or appeal routes. Approve deletion methods, provider coordination, backup handling and a minimal request record. No fixed deadline, backup expiry or irreversible-erasure guarantee has been supplied.

## Local files and external providers

A brief downloaded from this website stays on the visitor’s device or their chosen storage. Nexadly cannot delete that copy remotely. Unsent email drafts, browser autofill and device backups are managed through the visitor’s own applications and settings.

For ChatGPT account or hosting-platform information, consult [OpenAI’s privacy information](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/) and its request controls. The corporate mailbox is not an automatic deletion control for OpenAI, Google or an email provider.
