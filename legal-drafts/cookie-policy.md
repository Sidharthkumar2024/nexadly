Cookies are small pieces of data a service can store on a device. Other mechanisms, including browser storage and pixels, can also support site operation or tracking. Their purpose and handling depend on the actual implementation.

## What the application code currently does

The reviewed corporate application code has no cookie-setting logic, localStorage or sessionStorage persistence, custom analytics script or advertising pixel. The form temporarily uses entered details to prepare an email or create a downloadable text file.

A downloaded brief is a file chosen by the visitor, not a cookie. Browser autofill, caching, email drafts and device backups have their own controls. Absence of tracking code in this application does not establish absence of platform logs or cookies.

## Hosting and external fonts

The current ChatGPT Sites preview may use platform sign-in. OpenAI describes authentication and security uses in its [Cookie Policy](https://openai.com/policies/cookie-policy/). That general policy is a provider reference, not a verified list of cookies used by this specific preview.

The stylesheet loads Google Fonts from an external service. Google receives an IP address to deliver requested font files. See [Google’s explanation](https://fonts.googleblog.com/2022/11/your-privacy-and-google-fonts.html). No site-specific font retention or cookie conclusion is asserted here.

> **Review required:** Inspect requests and cookies on the chosen production domain and host, including a fresh visitor session and authenticated preview where relevant. Record name, provider, purpose, duration and category for each applicable technology. Decide whether to self-host fonts.

## Visitor choices and consent review

Visitors can review their browser’s cookie and site-data settings. Restricting provider authentication data may affect access to a private preview. Provider controls apply to the provider’s services; this site does not currently offer an in-app consent manager.

> **Review required:** Assess whether consent or other controls are required for the actual audience and technologies. If optional tracking is introduced, approve the categories and implement the required controls before it runs, including an accessible way to change a choice. Do not add an “Accept all” banner that has no working control behind it.
